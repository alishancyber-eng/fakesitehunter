About FakeSite Hunter

FakeSite Hunter is an AI-powered phishing and homograph detection platform. It helps users check suspicious links before opening them.

Our Mission

To protect users from fake websites, brand impersonation, and social engineering scams.

What We Built

  • Engine 1: URL and homograph analysis
  • Engine 2: Content + NLP analysis
  • Engine 3: Visual screenshot analysis

Why It Matters

Cyber attacks are increasing daily. A single fake URL can steal passwords, banking details, and private data. FakeSite Hunter provides a fast first defense.
